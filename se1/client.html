Book Rating Lookup

Service 1: Raw Book Rating

Enter a book title to find its rating out of 5.

Book Title Input Rules

  • Capitalization does not matter.
  • Spaces between words are allowed.
  • Extra spaces at the beginning or end are ignored.
  • Multiple spaces are treated as one space.
  • Spelling must match an existing book title.

Example: stardust = stardust = stardust

Available books: stardust, The Great Gatsby, The Hunger Games, Pride and Prejudice, The Hobbit.
